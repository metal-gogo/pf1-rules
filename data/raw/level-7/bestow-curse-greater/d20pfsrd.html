Bestow Curse

Contents

    • Bestow Curse, Greater
  • Alternate Bestow Curse Effects
  • More Alternate Curse Options
  • Alternative Curses

School necromancy [curse]; Level antipaladin 3, bloodrager 4, cleric/oracle 3, shaman 3, sorcerer/wizard 4, spiritualist 3, witch 3; Subdomain curse 3, tyranny 3; Bloodline accursed 4, div 4

CASTING

Casting Time 1 standard action
Components V, S

EFFECT

Range touch
Target creature touched
Duration permanent
Saving Throw Will negates; Spell Resistance yes

DESCRIPTION

You place a curse on the subject. Choose one of the following.

  • -6 decrease to an ability score (minimum 1).
  • -4 penalty on attack rolls, saves, ability checks, and skill checks.
  • Each turn, the target has a 50% chance to act normally; otherwise, it takes no action.

You may also invent your own curse, but it should be no more powerful than those described above.

The curse bestowed by this spell cannot be dispelled, but it can be removed with a break enchantment, limited wish, miracle, remove curse, or wish spell.

Bestow curse counters remove curse.

Bestow Curse, Greater

Source PAP82:SotS

School necromancy; Level cleric 7, sorcerer/wizard 8, witch 7; Subdomain corruption 6

DESCRIPTION

Greater bestow curse functions as above except with the following changes:

Choose one of the following.

  • –12 penalty to an ability score, or –6 penalty to two ability scores (to a minimum ability score of 1).
  • –8 penalty on attack rolls, saves, ability checks, and skill checks.
  • Each turn, the target has a 25% chance to act normally; otherwise, it takes no actions.
  • Bestow one of the curses or variants listed below.
  • Bestow curse of the ages or unluck.

If a specific affliction is bestowed, the save DC of the spell replaces the usual save DC of the affliction. You can also invent your own curse, but it should be no more powerful than those described above.

The curse bestowed by this spell cannot be dispelled, but it can be removed with a break enchantment, limited wish, miracle, or wish spell. It cannot be removed with remove curse or suppressed with abeyance. The DC to remove the curse with break enchantment increases by 5.

Alternate Bestow Curse Effects

Bestow curse allows the caster to invent a novel effect, but one no more powerful than those described in the spell itself (no worse than a 50% chance of losing actions, a –4 penalty on checks, or a –6 penalty to an ability score). Effects in line with that power level include the following.

  • When the victim is adjacent to the area of a damaging spell or spell-like effect, the area expands to include the victim.
  • The victim can’t heal naturally, and magical healing heals the victim by only half the usual amount (minimum 1 point). Fast healing and regeneration are likewise halved.
  • Whenever the victim takes damage, he is staggered for 1 round.
  • The victim is plagued by cacophonous sounds and strobing lights that only she can hear and see. She is distracted (+5 to Perception DCs), cannot take 10 on skill checks, and must succeed at a concentration check (DC 10 + spell level) to successfully cast spells.
  • Anytime the victim picks up or retrieves an object (including drawing a weapon or ammunition), there is a 50% chance that he immediately drops it. If ammunition is dropped, the attack being made is lost. Any remaining attacks in a full attack action may still be attempted.

More Alternate Curse Options

[Source]

When a spellcaster uses bestow curse, the following curses options can be substituted for those given in the spell description with GM approval.

  • Curse of Animal Anathema: Animals refuse to be within 5 feet of the target and do not respond to the target’s commands or requests.
  • Curse of Bungling: Each time the target attempts to help a friend or ally via an aid another action, there is a 50% chance the attempt fails and causes the ally to fail at the task.
  • Curse of Enemies Made: The next person introduced to the target for the first time will hate him or her uncontrollably forever (effectively treated as having a hostile attitude). Even if this curse is removed, the person still has a hostile attitude toward the subject of the curse, but the target can improve the person’s attitude normally with Diplomacy after the curse is removed.
  • Curse of Flesh Hunger: Anytime the subject of the curse is exposed to dead flesh, whether in a butcher shop, battlefield, or tomb, they have a 50% chance of being unable to resist the urge to consume the flesh for each new exposure. If they lose the 50% chance, they must use their turn to move toward and attempt to eat the dead flesh. This does not protect the subject of the curse from any diseases carried by such rotting meat and undead with flesh are considered ‘dead flesh’ for the purpose of this curse.
  • Curse of Glazed Eyes: All creatures of a specific kind are treated as being under the effects of a greater invisibility spell but only to the curse’s target. Invisibility purge does not help, but see invisibility and true seeing will work normally for the curse’s target. The source of the curse determines the kind of creature. The kind of creature must be specific, like humans, kytons or goblins, and not humanoids, dragons, etc.
  • Curse of Mistaken Identity: Each time the target meets someone for the first time, there is a 50% chance that the new person will confuse the target with a hated enemy, a well-known criminal, raving lunatic or some other individual antithetical to their beliefs.
  • Curse of Senselessness: Target is blind and deaf.
  • Curse of Sterility: Target is rendered sterile.
  • Curse of Victory Denied: Every time the target makes a d20 roll, a roll of a natural 20 is changed to a 1.
  • Curse of Violence: Each round in combat, there is a 25% chance that the target will attack the nearest creature rather than act normally.
  • Curse of Wracking Pain: If the subject of the curse performs a physical action, e.g. any skill check, ability check, attack roll, combat maneuver, or move action to the full extent of their single move speed, they are wracked with pain. The following round they are staggered with pain. This is a pain effect.
  • Curse of Ill-spent Years: The target gains the penalties to Strength, Dexterity and Constitution but not the bonuses to Intelligence, Wisdom Charisma that come with true aging.

The following effects can be substituted for those given in the lesser bestow curse description. These curses may also be used for bestow curse, though the penalties are doubled. These curses also may be used for minor bestow curse, but the penalties are halved (rounded down).

  • Curse of Blandness: All food and drink the target consumes is tasteless and bland. They are unable to benefit from spells or effects that grant bonuses based on the consumption of food and drink, and they do not gain any of the benefits of being drunk, only the penalties.

Alternative Curses

  • Curse of Broken Mirrors: When coming in contact with an object made of a glass or similar material material, the target must make a Will save (equal to the DC of the original curse) or be compelled to attempt to destroy the object.
  • Curse of Distraction: The target is unable to apply their caster level to concentration checks.
  • Curse of the Forgotten Fact: The target takes a -4 penalty on Knowledge or Lore checks.
  • Curse of the Offensive Smell: The target always smells a bit off to whomever they are close to. They suffer a -5 penalty on Bluff and Diplomacy skill checks or wild empathy checks with creatures who can smell.
  • Curse of the Unfocused Technique: The target takes a -5 to their CMD.
  • Curse of the Unforgiven: Any act that contradicts the current alignment of the target causes their alignment to shift.
  • Curse of the Unfortunate Step: The target takes a -5 penalty on Stealth checks that rely on sound. They manage to step on every creaky board, twig, or loose stone they possibly can.

The following effects can be substituted for those given in the greater bestow curse description.

  • Curse of Antimagic: The target is unable to cast spells, use spell-like abilities, or activate spell completion or spell trigger items.
  • Curse of Animal Hostility: All animals and magical beasts with Intelligence scores of 3 or lower are automatically hostile toward the subject of the curse and will attack them, flee, or negatively impact them in any way possible.
  • Curse of Sensory Deprivation: Target loses all of their senses like sight, hearing, smell, tremorsense, blindsense, etc.
  • Curse of Dull Metal: Valuable metals (such as platinum, gold, silver, and copper) turn to lead in the target’s possession, even if they are in a bag of holding or stored away from the target. The target’s touch transmutes valuable metals (including coins) into lead as well.
  • Curse of Friendly Virulence: A random ally or loved one of the target contracts a disease. If the disease is magically cured or runs its course (regardless of the outcome), another qualifying individual contracts a new disease.
  • Curse of Interminable Bungling: Anytime the subject of the curse attempts to use an aid another action, it automatically fails and causes the ally to fail their check.
  • Curse of Memory Blotting: No one can remember the target of the curse and they cannot make new memories about them. They are a permanent stranger to everyone. It manifests in interesting ways, for example, a subject of this curse could give a task to another, that person would remember the task but not who gave them it or who to return to when it is done.
  • Curse of Knowing You: 1d4+ 1 of the target’s loved ones or allies are affected by a curse chosen from the bestow curse list above. The affected loved ones can be chosen randomly or, if known by the creator of the curse, directed.
  • Curse of Shunning: All of the target’s loved ones and allies suddenly despise him and are considered to have unfriendly attitudes. Once the curse is lifted, the unfriendly attitude remains but may be improved as normal via Diplomacy.
