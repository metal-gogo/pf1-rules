## Outcome

[C] The pinned Foundry snapshot contains 3,028 spell records. The pre-reconciliation local database contained 3,024 canonical spells. Normalized identity matching paired 2,994 names directly and 26 more through explicit title or numbering aliases. Six Foundry names were genuine AoN identities absent locally. They are now ingested, bringing the local canonical count to 3,030 and mapping every Foundry spell identity.

[C] Foundry asserted 189 class/level rows absent from the pre-reconciliation canonical set. Fourteen had already been resolved in the earlier secondary-catalog review. This pass classified the remaining 175 rows as follows:

- 38 additional class memberships had exact d20PFSRD spell-page corroboration and were added under the reviewed class-union policy.
- 25 rows assigned a different level to a class already printed by AoN. The canonical records keep AoN's level; no duplicate level was added.
- 112 rows across 100 spells are asserted only by Foundry in the available evidence. d20PFSRD does not print any competing level for those rows, but it also does not corroborate them. They remain pending rather than becoming silent canonical overrides.
